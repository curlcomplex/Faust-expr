# Perc source register — 10 September 2026

## Manufacturer behavior

Elektron Syntakt manual, OS 1.30 edition, PC Carbon text on printed pages 89–90 (publisher content mirrored at https://manuals.plus/m/c8781a2f37da076182fea0b38d398320dfca541b75d9cc0133272410d5c772ae.pdf). The control roles are Tune, Sweep, Punch, Decay, Inharmonicity, Modulation, Modulation Envelope and Overdrive. The manual describes Sweep as coupled depth/time and Punch as adding transient emphasis/distortion. These descriptions are not an oscillator diagram or numeric macro law. The direct current support endpoint returned 403 during this pass; no current-firmware verification or fresh hardware capture is claimed.

Model:Cycles Perc remains the conceptual compact-machine family. PC Carbon recordings are separately named comparators, not evidence that both products use identical DSP. Our pitch/range/ratios and the continuous Punch mapping are authored choices, not recovered hardware scalings.

## Licensed audio

Winston Edwards / Particles Into Waves, *Syntakt Designer Drums*, released 13 June 2022:
https://particlesintowaves.bandcamp.com/album/syntakt-designer-drums

The creator identifies PC Carbon among the digital machines, describes dry/no-post captures with onboard drive on some hits, and publishes the pack under CC BY 4.0. The fixed set here is PCC-01/02/04/05/07/08 plus PCC-03/09. These are the public MP3 previews, decoded to mono float32/48 kHz, not the original lossless download. Original/decoded file hashes, source HTML, conversion and attribution accompany the evidence package. Nothing is sampled into the instrument.

Firmware, exact controls, pitch/velocity/gate settings, gain and per-hit drive state remain unknown. Therefore the recordings support exploratory broad time/spectral comparisons only. All clips were inspected by the original measurement pass; the reserved labels are not an untouched holdout after that exposure. No clone score, exact source recovery or creator endorsement.

Raw audio remains unchanged. The reference-nearest audition crops the first up-to-1.5 seconds only for listening, applies one documented whole-excerpt RMS gain to each synthesized clip and a shared attenuation; the quantitative comparison uses a full 10-second horizon.
