# Prior art and listening references — 10 September 2026

## Nord Lead 4
https://www.nordkeyboards.com/legacy-products/nord-lead-4/
Official specifications list 128 oscillator-1 wavetables and True Unison. This is the design precedent for an immediately playable spectral source plus detuned same-note oscillators. Nord's branded Morph is a multi-parameter performance assignment, not evidence that our frame-scanning law matches theirs.

Official audio: https://soundcloud.com/nordkeyboards/sets/nord-lead-4-performance
Five manufacturer demos (NL4 Demo 1–5 FS), described as live performances with no overdubbing/external processing except where noted. Internal effects/routing remain uncontrolled. The playlist is all-rights-reserved. These are linked listening references, NOT redistributed calibration samples.

## Nord Wave 2
https://www.nordkeyboards.com/products/nord-wave-2/

## What was and was not used
The pages and available track metadata were inspected. The Nord audio was not downloaded, measured or used for parameter fitting in this pass, and no claim of listening-based emulation or recovered topology is made. These materials demonstrate suitable public listening targets; they are not controlled reference data. No assets or names imply endorsement by Nord, Elektron or Surge.

Our source frames, their ordering, saturation and interpolation are independently authored. Eight clean and eight spectrally saturated single cycles are generated from explicit formulas. The runtime evaluates their 64 sine coefficients analytically; it is not a sample player and does not import tables from a manufacturer's firmware. The fixed coefficients and cycle hashes are part of the sound identity.

## Comparison against Tone
The package contains the same three-note phrase through Tone and single-oscillator Morph with no unison. Pitch, gate times and release time constant match; one whole-phrase RMS gain is documented, and attack curves differ. The selected Tone patches are reasonable contrasting examples, not an exhaustive optimizer search. The comparison cannot prove that no Tone setting can produce any individual Morph timbre. The intended distinction is direct, smooth traversal through a coherent harmonic spectrum, with same-note stacking as an additional feature rather than its only distinction.

Felix's acceptance remains the musical go/no-go gate. No invented similarity percentage is substituted for that choice.
